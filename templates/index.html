End-to-End Workflow

The Complete OPD Healthcare Journey

A seamless 4-phase clinical path connecting patients, medical staff, doctors, and healthcare facilities.

1

Role Authentication

Secure, role-based authentication directing users to their dedicated dashboard (Patient, Doctor, Hospital, or Admin).

2

Facility & Doctor Discovery

Explore multi-system facilities, verified clinical departments, and specialist doctors across AYUSH & Modern Medicine.

3

AI Case Intake & Triage

Voice or text input of chief complaints followed by exactly two contextual clinical questions and preliminary triage assessment.

4

OPD Token & Live Queue

Confirm your token, check real-time queue status, and enter the doctor's consultation with a prepared clinical brief.

ABDM Standards & Privacy

Your Health Data Remains Secure & Private

Engineered according to National Digital Health Mission standards to ensure consent, confidentiality, and data sovereignty.

🔒

Zero Audio Storage

Voice input uses client-side browser speech recognition. No raw audio is permanently stored on external servers.

🛡️

Role-Based Isolation

Strict server-side authorization boundaries guarantee patients, doctors, and hospitals only access authorized data.

📜

Consent-Driven Sharing

Longitudinal health records and clinical history are shared exclusively with explicit patient approval.
